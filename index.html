05 — Process
  1. Audit
  2. Plan
  3. Build
  4. Ship
  5. Support

01

Audit

Understand what is actually there.

I read the codebase, reproduce the problem, and find the real cause rather than the first symptom. You get a plain summary of what is wrong and what fixing it takes.

02

Plan

Break it into milestones.

Scope, estimates and priorities, ordered so something useful ships early. You see the trade-offs, which keeps the decisions about time and cost yours rather than mine.

03

Build

Write it, then test it.

Features developed against real data and tested before they reach you. I use AI tooling to move faster, and I review, secure and understand everything I ship with it.

04

Ship

Into production, carefully.

Deployment, servers, SSL, caching and CDN. Releases are staged so problems are caught in advance, instead of your users being the ones who find them first.

05

Support

Stay on after launch.

Maintenance, upgrades and the occasional emergency. Work stays documented as it goes, so you are never locked in to me — you keep me because it is useful.
