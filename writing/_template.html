The standfirst. One or two sentences saying what problem this solves and for whom, so a reader can decide in five seconds whether the rest is for them.

Opening paragraph: the problem, concretely. What was happening, what it cost, and why the obvious fix did not work.

Body copy. Prose over bullet lists wherever the ideas actually connect — a list of five fragments is usually a paragraph that has not been written yet.
